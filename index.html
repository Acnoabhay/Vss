Who we are

A community with a purpose.

Vidyarthi Seva Sangathan (VSS) is a student-led initiative associated with the CGIT Raipur student community. It aims to create a constructive space where students can connect, contribute, learn and help each other navigate campus life.

Student supportPeer learningLeadershipCommunity

“The strength of a campus is not only in its classrooms, but also in how its students stand together.”

VSS encourages students to take initiative, share knowledge, build relationships across batches and create experiences that make college life more connected and meaningful.

Our approachPractical, collaborative and student-focused.
Our communityStudents learning and contributing together.
Our spiritRespect, responsibility and participation.
Our promiseMake opportunities easier to access and share.
Our purpose

What VSS stands for

VSS is designed to be more than a name. It is a shared effort to make student life more informed, welcoming and collaborative.

◎

Student guidance

Encouraging peer-to-peer guidance, sharing useful information and helping students find the right people or resources when they need support.

↗

Leadership & initiative

Creating opportunities for students to take responsibility, coordinate activities, develop confidence and learn by doing.

⌘

Collaboration

Connecting students across branches, batches and interests so that ideas, skills and experiences can be shared.

✦

Campus engagement

Supporting meaningful cultural, educational and community activities that make campus life more active and memorable.

♡

Service mindset

Promoting a culture of helping without expecting recognition, and putting collective progress ahead of individual credit.

▤

Knowledge sharing

Making it easier to pass on practical lessons, opportunities and campus know-how from one student group or batch to another.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A quick introduction to VSS and how students can get involved.

What is VSS?

VSS stands for Vidyarthi Seva Sangathan — a student-led initiative guided by the principle “By the students, for the students.”

Who can participate?

Students interested in contributing to student support, guidance, events and community activities can express their interest through the official VSS contact channels.

Do I need prior experience to volunteer?

No prior experience is necessary for many volunteer roles. A willingness to learn, cooperate and take responsibility is a good starting point.

Does VSS replace official college authorities?

No. VSS is a student-led initiative and does not replace the institute administration, official student bodies or authorised college communication channels.

How can alumni contribute?

Professionals and alumni may contribute through experience-sharing and mentoring. VSS envisions weekly online mentorship and monthly on-campus seminars; schedules will be shared when confirmed.

Get in touch

Let's build something meaningful.

Have an idea, want to volunteer or wish to collaborate? Reach out through the verified VSS contact channels.
