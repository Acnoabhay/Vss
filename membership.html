About VSS

Vidyarthi Seva Sangathan is an independent, unofficial student-led community. Our mission is to connect students, make peer guidance easier, encourage volunteering and support student-led initiatives. Our vision is a connected, informed and supportive campus community where students learn from one another and grow together.

By the students, for the students.
◎

What members can do

Join activities, share opportunities, seek peer guidance, contribute ideas, volunteer in programmes and help build a respectful campus network.

Participation is voluntary and subject to applicable institute rules.

STUDENT AWARENESS

Know your rights. Respect your responsibilities.

Quick reference

Student protections & avenues

  • Be treated with dignity and without unlawful discrimination or harassment.
  • Access applicable institutional grievance-redressal channels and receive fair consideration under the relevant rules.
  • Study in an environment free from ragging, intimidation and violence.
  • Consult official academic regulations, examination rules and published institutional policies.

Shared responsibilities

  • Respect fellow students, faculty, staff, campus property and differing viewpoints.
  • Follow current attendance, examination, hostel, safety and discipline rules issued by competent authorities.
  • Never participate in ragging, bullying, harassment, discrimination or violence.
  • Use VSS platforms responsibly; protect personal information and do not circulate unverified claims.

This is general awareness information, not legal advice or a complete statement of rights. Constitutional Fundamental Rights apply according to the Constitution and law; Article 51A lists citizens’ Fundamental Duties. Institute-specific rules and current CSVTU ordinances take precedence for campus procedures.
